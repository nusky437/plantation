About Peoples Development Plantation

Leading sustainable agriculture in Sri Lanka with innovation and community focus

Founded with a vision to revolutionize Sri Lankan agriculture, Peoples Development Plantation combines traditional farming wisdom with modern sustainable practices. We empower local communities through fair trade, innovative cultivation techniques, and environmental stewardship.
